06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is OKX Card free for everyone?

No universal free issuance claim can be made. Eligibility and current issuance fees vary by region and account. Check the terms displayed in your own app.

Can I get 50% cashback on subscriptions?

The 50% cashback is a conditional promotion, not a universal ongoing reward. Check current promotion terms, eligible merchants, supported countries and the reward cap before paying.

Can I use Vodafone Cash or InstaPay?

These payment methods appear in the Egyptian tutorial. Availability and P2P counterparties are location-dependent. Follow only the supported methods shown in your account.

Can I change my billing country to another country?

Use correct billing details matching your verified information. Changing your country to a false address can cause payment or verification issues.
